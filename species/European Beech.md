## Repot (every 2 years)
- id: t1
- category: repot
- start: 05-01
- end: 05-21

Repot in a basic soil mix as buds swell. Norwegian springs run 3–4 weeks behind the UK, so go by bud movement, not the date.

## Feed — trees still developing
- id: t2
- category: feed
- start: 05-10
- end: 08-20

If trunk or branch structure is still being built, feed from the moment leaves unfurl right through the growing season.

## Fortnightly feeding — refined trees
- id: t3
- category: feed
- start: 06-05
- end: 08-20

Refined trees get a 3–4 week feeding hold after leaf-out to keep spring growth fine, then feed fortnightly until late summer.

## Prune to bud
- id: t4
- category: prune
- start: 06-15
- end: 07-15

Once spring growth has fully extended, cut back to a bud facing the direction you want new growth to take.

## Wiring — check often
- id: t5
- category: wire
- start: 06-16
- end: 09-15

Branches thicken fast now. Beech bark is smooth and scars easily, so inspect wire every few days while it's on.

## Air-layering window
- id: t6

- start: 07-05
- end: 08-05

Take air-layers once this year's spring growth has hardened off.

Midsummer is an alternative to late-winter hard pruning — wounds heal fastest now, so it's also the best time to remove large branches.

## Late-winter hard pruning window
- id: t12
- category: prune
- start: 02-15
- end: 03-20

The alternative to midsummer hard pruning — do this before spring growth resumes.

## Position & winter protection
- id: t13
- category: other
- start: 10-20
- end: 04-15

Beech takes sun or light shade, but keep it out of harsh drying wind — the thin leaves crisp at the edges. Move it into a cold frame, unheated greenhouse or sheltered, mulched spot before hard frosts: the species is rated hardy across northern Europe in the ground (RHS H6), but a potted rootball is a different matter, and the real risk here is spring rather than midwinter — PFAF notes young leaves and flowers are readily damaged by late frosts, so keep protection to hand until bud break is safely past. Browned foliage often hangs on all winter, sheltering next year's buds; leave it.

## Pre-bud-break pruning window
- id: t14
- category: prune
- start: 03-20
- end: 04-20

Bonsai Empire's timing for larger branches — prune in spring before the new buds open. In Norway that sits just after the late-winter window above, so in practice the two run together into one long dormant-season opportunity ending the moment buds move.
